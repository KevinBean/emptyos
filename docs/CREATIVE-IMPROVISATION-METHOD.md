# Improvisation pass for content creation

**Status: optional experiment.** This is a way to explore the middle of a piece when its starting point and intended effect are known. It has not been shown to improve EmptyOS writing, songs, or music videos. Keep the normal creation path available and judge the result in the finished draft or living master.

## The method

1. **Name the frame.** Write the starting image, line, sound, or situation; the intended feeling or change at the end; and any fixed constraints (song audio, character, point of view, visual style, length, budget).
2. **Make an offer.** Add one concrete action, image, phrase, sound, or obstacle that responds to what is already present. Do not jump to an unrelated attractive idea.
3. **Follow the consequence.** Ask: *Because of that addition, what becomes possible, harder, or newly meaningful?* Continue with a small chain of offers. An offer may adopt, augment, or contrast the previous one.
4. **Return with a change.** Bring back a distinctive early element near the end, if it can now mean something different. A return is useful only when the intervening events earn it.
5. **Select and revise.** Keep the chain that makes the intended effect clearest. Remove offers that merely decorate or repeat. The creator decides what enters the actual draft, lyrics, treatment, or timeline.

An offer can serve one of four jobs: **reveal** something already latent in the start; **complicate** the goal; **vary** a motif in sound, language, or image; or **reframe** an earlier detail. These jobs are working prompts, not mandatory story beats. If a proposed ending stops fitting what the exploration discovers, the creator can change it deliberately.

## Use in each medium

| Medium | Cheap improvisation surface | What to carry into production |
|---|---|---|
| Fiction or other creative writing | A short chain of scene beats or paragraphs. Test what each action causes and what the character notices. | Selected beats, language, and an ending that pays off an earlier detail. |
| Songwriting | Several spoken or sung takes over a fixed pulse or chord loop. Vary one lyric, melodic cell, or arrangement gesture at a time. | Phrases and motifs that carry the emotional change; then edit for singability and form. |
| Music video | Treatment notes and a full-song placeholder living master. Try images or cuts at each section without generating paid footage. | A reviewed sequence whose visual changes support the song and whose repeated images gain meaning. |

The existing **Improvisation Center** (`/improv/`) brings these three explorations together under **Create**. It uses the same optional pass as the in-context panels in Fiction Engine, Music Library, and Music Studio. Its **Stage**, **Warm-ups**, and **Library** areas still serve performance practice; the Library stores performance scenes, not creative-path drafts. Copy a chosen creative path into the relevant project app to develop it. The center does not silently save or insert suggestions into a work.

For MVs, this pass fits **inside stage 2 treatment exploration and stage 3 living master v0**, before the approval and spending gates in [MV-PRODUCTION-GUIDE.md](MV-PRODUCTION-GUIDE.md). It never changes the selected song, bypasses the living master, or approves footage.
The MV suggestion panel passes the song's lyrics and selected audio file as context; it does not listen to the recording or infer beat timing. Check each proposed image against the actual song before using it.

## Check whether it helps

The output of the pass is a **candidate path**, not proof of quality. After trying it, compare the candidate with a direct outline or draft from the same starting frame. Let the creator judge both blind if practical, then record:

- Which version better conveys the intended feeling or change?
- Which details feel causally or emotionally connected, and which feel attached only for novelty?
- Was the time spent worthwhile? Did the method create a useful idea that would otherwise have been missed?

Keep examples of both successes and failures. A few favorable sessions establish usefulness for those sessions, not a general effect. Avoid automatically applying the method to every project until there is repeated creator preference for its results.

The app panels record only the creator's comparison (`better`, `same`, or `worse`) and whether the path was made manually or with AI. They do not store the creative material or prove a causal improvement. The comparison is a lightweight signal for deciding whether to run a more careful trial later.

The AI panel asks for two paths so the creator has a choice, and three to five steps so each path is short enough to inspect while still showing a consequence and a return. Input limits and an exploratory model setting keep this a bounded drafting aid. These are design choices, not measured optimal values; revise them if trials show they constrain useful ideas.

## Research basis and limits

- Sawyer describes improvisers working within shared structures and extending one another's small offers, rather than creating without a frame: [*Improvisational Cultures*](https://doi.org/10.1207/S15327884MCA0703_05) and [*Educating for Innovation* (2006)](https://keithsawyer.com/PDFs/Thinkjournal.pdf).
- In interviews while reviewing recordings, 15 free improvisers reported maintaining or changing their contribution by adopting, augmenting, or contrasting others: [Wilson and MacDonald (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1177/0305735615606527). This is evidence about music performance, not a validated songwriting or MV procedure.
- An interactive-drama study found reincorporation improved structural unity but did not improve players' reported story experience: [Tomaszewski (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1609/aiide.v7i2.12468). A callback alone is not evidence of emotional success.
- Improvisation-trained musicians scored higher on divergent-thinking measures in one comparative study, but training and self-selection are hard to separate: [Kleinmintz et al. (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0101568). The proposed creation method and its effect in EmptyOS remain untested.
