🎙 Dictation

Click into any EmptyOS text field and a 🎤 button appears — click it and speak. Your words are transcribed, lightly polished (punctuation, casing, your custom terms kept), and inserted at the caret. Speak to create, type to refine — it never takes the keyboard away from you.

Dictation is off

Turn on Enable Dictation in the ⚙ settings panel (takes effect immediately), or set feature.enabled = true under [apps.dictation] in emptyos.toml.

Active everywhere

Dictation works on every EmptyOS page. To use it:

  1. Click into any text field, textarea, or editor — a 🎤 button appears at its corner.
  2. Click 🎤 (it turns red) and speak — the words appear live in the field as you talk.
  3. Click 🎤 again to stop; the text is then polished in place (punctuation, casing, your terms kept).

Prefer keys? A push-to-talk hotkey also works — hold ctrl+shift+d while in a field. (Set it in ⚙ Settings; pick a combo your OS/browser doesn't already use.)
